The problem

One injury can change an entire season.

Players face collisions, falls, sprints, sudden stops and repeated high load. Sometimes a player keeps playing after a hit, even though the way they move has already changed.

The solution

Meet PlaySafe

PlaySafe combines smart sportswear, movement sensors and AI-powered analysis to help football teams understand what is happening with their players in real time.

How PlaySafe works

How AI v1 decides

PlaySafe v1 does not guess injuries. It learns each player's normal from earlier sessions and measures how far today's movement is from it. Move the slider and switch players.

The PlaySafe jersey

Select a sensor

Two small removable modules on the back: in a shirt pocket between the shoulder blades and on the shorts waistband. No sensors on the front.

Future / optional Later: heart-rate chest strap, temperature sensor.

Potential risk, not diagnosis

Movement change after impact

PlaySafe compares each player with their own normal movement and flags clear changes after an impact. It does not predict injuries, give a diagnosis or locate an injury in a specific body part.

Why PlaySafe?

PlaySafe is a sports monitoring and early-warning system. It does not replace professional medical diagnosis or medical staff. AI identifies unusual movement patterns and potential risk, and recommends professional assessment.
